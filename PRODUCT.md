# Product

## Register
brand

## Product
Vexoraa is a music marketing agency. It grows independent artists on Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud and Apple Music, with a focus on placements in major editorial-grade playlists whose audiences run from 500K to 2M+ monthly listeners.

## Users
Independent artists, managers and small labels who have music out and are stuck at a few hundred to a few thousand monthly listeners. They open the site on a phone at night, between sessions, sceptical of "growth services" because most are bot farms.

## Purpose of the site (Persuade)
Get an artist to book a strategy call. The visitor must believe in seconds that Vexoraa places music in real playlists with real listeners, and that the results are specific and repeatable.

## Brand
Logo: a black calligraphic V whose white swooshes read as sound waves. Black and white at its core. Premium, cinematic, confident. Never hype-y, never "growth hack".

## Anti-references
Bot-farm "buy streams" sites, neon-on-black crypto looks, stock photos of headphones, gradient SaaS templates.

## Constraints / assumptions (inferred from the brief, no interview run)
- Stack pinned by the user: Next.js, Three.js, GSAP, repo `muhammadshaheer0123/Vexoraa-Marketing`.
- Testimonials, case studies, pricing and metrics are DEMO content written to look realistic; they must be replaced with real data before launch.
- No form backend exists; the contact form opens a prefilled email.
